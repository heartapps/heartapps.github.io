    Getting started

    Install Mentor Heart, register with your D.No. or staff ID, and learn the main screens.

    • Students
    • Professors
    • Admins

    Mentor Heart is for the students and professors of a department. Only people on the department’s list can register. The department admin checks and approves each new account. This keeps strangers out.

    Get the app

    Choose the one for your phone:

    • Android phone: install Mentor Heart from Google Play. It updates itself.
    • iPhone: there is no App Store app. Use the web app at mh.inigo.co.in and add it to your Home Screen. See how.
    • Computer: open mh.inigo.co.in in any browser and sign in.
    Note Some people installed the Android app from the APK file on this website. That app still works, and it updates itself from Settings › Updates. You don’t need to change anything.

    If the APK won’t install

    Does your phone say App not installed, Invalid package or There was a problem parsing the package? Check these, one by one:

    1. Your Android version. The app needs Android 7.0 or newer. To check, open your phone’s Settings › About phone and look at Android version. On Android 6 or older, the app can’t install. Use the web app at mh.inigo.co.in instead. It does nearly everything the app does.
    2. Mentor Heart from Google Play. Is Mentor Heart already on the phone from Google Play? Then the APK can’t replace it. Keep the Play Store app; it updates itself. To use the APK instead, uninstall the Play Store app first. Your records are safe on the server, but save your Calm journal first with Save a backup file.
    3. The download. The file must be complete. Open this website in Chrome, not inside WhatsApp or Instagram, and download the APK again. It is about 55 MB. A file sent on WhatsApp can arrive broken, or with a name like mentorheart.apk.bin.
    4. Free space. Keep at least 200 MB free on the phone. Some phones say Invalid package when the space is too low.
    Tip Your phone has Google Play? Then install from Google Play. It is the same app, and it avoids these problems.

    Register as a student

    • Students

    First, your D.No. must be on your department’s list. Your professors or the admin add it.

    1. Open Mentor Heart and tap New here? Register.
    2. Under I am a, tap Student.
    3. Choose your Department. Then find Your D.No. in the list. Your class is filled in for you.
    4. Type your Full name as in college records.
    5. Make a Password with at least 8 letters or numbers.
    6. Type your Mobile number. Email is optional. Only your professors see it.
    7. Add your Date of birth. If you are under 18, also add a Parent or guardian name and mobile.
    8. You can add a Profile photo and a few lines in About you. This helps your mentor.
    9. Tick I agree that my department keeps these details for mentoring and academic work. Then send the form.
    Tip Your D.No. is not in the list? It may not be added yet. Or someone already used it. Ask your class professor or the department admin.

    Register as a professor

    • Professors
    1. Tap New here? Register. Under I am a, tap Professor.
    2. Choose your Department. Then choose Your name on the staff list. If your name is not there, choose I am not on the list.
    3. Type your Staff ID, for example 20CBU51. You sign in with it.
    4. Type your Initials as they appear on the timetable, for example IPV. This links you to your hours.
    5. Add your full name, a password (at least 8 characters), your mobile number and your Designation. Then agree and send.

    If your staff ID is different from the one on the staff list, the app tells you. Check it, or ask the admin.

    Set up a new department

    • Professors
    • Admins

    Is your department not in Mentor Heart yet? One of its professors sets it up. That professor becomes its first admin.

    1. Ask the app’s owner for the new-department code (write to Inigo from the home page). The code stops strangers from making departments.
    2. Register as a professor. Under Department, choose My department is not listed.
    3. Type the Department name, the Short name (for example BBA) and the College. Then type the code.

    Is your department in the list but it says No admin yet? Choose it. Turn on Make me this department’s admin. Then type the department code from the owner.

    Wait for approval

    After you register, you see three steps: Registered, Admin approves, You’re in. The admin usually approves you within a day.

    • Tap Check again to see if you are approved.
    • Not approved: speak to the department office.
    • Turned off: your account was switched off. Speak to the department office.

    Sign in

    Type your D.No. or staff ID and your Password. Then tap Sign in.

    Forgot your password? There is no email reset. Ask the department admin. They can give you a new password.

    The main screens

    There are five tabs at the bottom of the app:

    • Today: today’s day order, the class now, and the next class.
    • Calendar: the day order of each date, and events, exams and holidays.
    • Timetable: your class’s week, or the hours you teach.
    • Mentoring: your mentor, or your mentees.
    • More: everything else. Leave, studies, announcements, settings and help.

    The first time you open the app, it shows a short introduction. To see it again, open More › Settings › Show the introduction again. There is also help inside the app, in More › Help.
