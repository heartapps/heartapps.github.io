    Help › Running the department

    Admin tools

    Approve new accounts, keep the lists, classes, mentors, day order and bell timings up to date, reset passwords, and handle message reports.

    • Admins
    • Professors

    The admin is a professor of the department. The admin tools are in More › Admin. A department can have more than one admin.

    People lists: who can register

    • Admins
    • Professors

    Only people on the department’s lists can register. Every professor can add people from More › People lists.

    Students: choose the class. Then add the D.No.s in one of these ways:

    • a range, for example 26UBU601-669;
    • a list copied from a spreadsheet column;
    • an Excel or CSV file.

    You can leave out D.No.s of students who left. You see a preview before anything is added.

    Alumni: choose the batch, for example 2021-24. Then add the D.No.s the same way. They can then register as alumni. Tap Excel for the list, with who has registered.

    Staff: add each professor’s name as the timetable shows it, with their initials (for example IPV). You can also add their staff ID. If you do, they must register with that ID. If someone registered with I am not on the list, tap their name on the list and choose Link to an account.

    From other departments: under Staff, add the Tamil, English, EVS or Human Excellence teachers who teach your classes. They show on the timetable but get no account here. Class reps can add them too.

    Note The app can’t read old .xls files. Save the file as .xlsx or .csv first.

    Approve new accounts

    • Admins
    1. Open More › New registrations. The number shows how many people are waiting.
    2. Check each person’s details. Under 18 marks students who are under 18.
    3. Tap Approve. Or tap Reject if they are not from your department. Delete removes the sign-up.

    Approve all approves everyone on the list at once. A student’s class comes from their D.No.

    Reset a password

    • Admins

    There is no email reset. To give someone a new password:

    1. Open More › All users and tap the person.
    2. Under Forgot password?, type a New password with at least 8 characters.
    3. Tap Set new password.
    4. Tell the person their new password, face to face.

    Change a person’s account

    • Admins

    In All users, tap a person. You can change their name, mobile number, class, mentor or designation. On the same page you can also:

    • turn on Admin (only for professors). Admins approve people, edit timetables and choose mentors;
    • turn on Head of department (see Name a head of department);
    • set the Account to On, Turned off, Rejected or Waiting;
    • Delete account. This can’t be undone.

    Classes, sections and class reps

    • Admins

    Open More › Classes and sections.

    • Add classes: type the Programme (for example BBA, B.Com or MBA). Type the Sections (for example A-D for four sections). Leave it empty if the year has only one class.
    • Rename or Delete a class: open the class. If you delete a class, its timetable and class posts are deleted too. Its students keep their accounts, but they have no class.
    • Class representatives: choose up to two for each class. They can post to their class.

    Mentor allotments

    • Admins

    Open More › Mentor allotments to give students a mentor.

    • Pick students: choose the mentor, then tick the students.
    • Whole section: give one mentor a whole section. Turn on Students who join later get this mentor too if you want new students to get the same mentor. Turn on Also move students who have a mentor to change their mentor too.

    Day order

    • Admins

    Open More › Day order.

    • Follow the college calendar: use the calendar the owner put in the app. Or set Counting starts on: the first working day of the term.
    • Name day orders A to F: turn it off to use Day 1 to Day 6.
    • Saturdays are working days: turn it off if the college has no classes on Saturdays.
    • Change one date: choose the Date. Choose No classes or a day order. Add a Note. Tap Add change. Next two weeks shows the result.

    Tap Save day order. Everyone sees the new day order at once.

    Bell timings and breaks

    • Admins

    Open More › Bell timings. Set the start and end time of each period, and the breaks. When you add a break, the app can move the later periods for you. You must set bell timings before anyone can fill the timetable.

    Timetable and electives

    • Admins

    The admin can edit any class’s timetable from Timetable. See Edit the weekly timetable and Subjects. Electives are in More › Electives. See Electives.

    The mentoring report

    • Admins

    More › Mentoring report counts the meetings of each mentor and mentee. Choose the last 30 days, 90 days, or all time. Share it as a PDF, an Excel (CSV) file or text. Private notes are never in it.

    Best student award

    • Admins

    More › Best student makes a Scorecard for each year from the app’s data.

    • Under Weights, choose how much each thing counts, from 0 to 10. For example, tasks done on time.
    • Nominations shows the students that mentors suggest, with their reasons.
    • Tap Choose as best student to record the winner.

    Department settings

    • Admins

    More › Department settings has the department’s name, short name, college and academic year.

    When everyone has registered, turn off Registration open. Then strangers can’t sign up. Alumni on the alumni list can still register.

    Alumni mentoring for students lets students ask the department’s alumni for mentoring and chat with them. Professors see who mentors whom, never the messages. See Alumni mentors.

    A new academic year

    • Admins

    Once a year, open Department settings › New academic year. Here you name the new year, set when classes start, move each class up a year, and clear the timetable. You see every change before it happens.

    Final-year students become alumni is on at first. They keep their account and see the alumni app. Turn it off to turn their accounts off instead.

    Message reports

    • Admins

    When someone reports a message or a person in Messages, the admins get a notification. Open More › Message reports.

    • You see only the message reported and the two before it. Never the rest of the chat.
    • Talk to the people involved. Tap Mark handled and write in a line what you did.
    • To stop someone using the app, turn off their account. Everyone can also block anyone themselves.

    Rooms

    • Admins

    The admins approve new alumni meets and can close a room or delete any message. See Approve and moderate.

    The only admin

    • Admins

    If you are the only admin, you can’t delete your account. First make another professor an admin in All users.
