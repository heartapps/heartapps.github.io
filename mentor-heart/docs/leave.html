    Leave

    Apply for leave or on-duty, with a photo of the certificate. Mentors and admins decide in the app.

    • Students
    • Mentors
    • Admins

    Apply for leave

    • Students
    1. Open More and tap the Leave tile. Then tap Apply for leave.
    2. Choose the Type of leave: Medical, Personal, On duty (OD), Family or Something else.
    3. Choose the first day (From) and the last day (To).
    4. Choose Full day, Forenoon or Afternoon. The app counts the working days. It skips Sundays and the department’s holidays.
    5. Write the Reason. One line is enough.
    6. If you have a medical certificate or a letter, add a photo of it. Only your mentor and the department admin see it.
    7. Tap Send to my mentor.

    You get a notification when your leave is decided.

    If you don’t have a mentor yet, the button says Send to the department. Then the admin decides.

    See or cancel your leave

    • Students

    Tap the Leave tile to see all your leave applications and how many days you have taken this term.

    • You can withdraw (cancel) leave that is still waiting.
    • You can also withdraw approved leave before it starts.

    Leave is kept as a record. It is never deleted.

    Decide leave

    • Mentors
    • Admins

    The mentor decides. The admin can decide too.

    • Waiting leave shows in Needs you on the Mentoring tab, on Today, and in More › Leave applications.
    • Open it. Look at the dates, the reason and the photo. Then tap Decide.

    The student gets a notification. While leave is waiting, professors get a note at 9 am. (You can turn this off in Settings.)

    Questions about leave

    Why does it say “No working days”?

    The dates you chose are Sundays or department holidays. So there is nothing to apply for.

    Can I apply for more than 120 days?

    No. Apply in parts.

    Who sees my certificate photo?

    Your mentor and the department admin. Nobody else.
