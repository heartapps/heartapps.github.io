    Signing in and your account

    I forgot my password.

    Ask your department admin. They can set a new password for you in the app. There is no email reset. See Reset a password.

    The app says my D.No. or staff ID is wrong.

    Check that you typed it the same way as on your ID card, such as 26UBU601 or 20CBU51. If it is still wrong, ask your department admin.

    I can’t find my D.No. in the list when I register.

    Your D.No. is not on the department’s list yet, or someone already registered with it. Ask your class professor or the department admin to add it.

    It says “This ID already has an account”.

    Someone has already registered with this D.No. or staff ID. If it was you, sign in. If it wasn’t you, tell your department admin.

    I registered. When can I use the app?

    After the department admin approves you. This is usually within a day. Open the app and tap Check again.

    My registration was not approved.

    Speak to the department office. The admin may not have known you were in the department.

    My department is not in the list.

    A professor of your department must set it up first. See Set up a new department.

    How do I change my D.No., staff ID or class?

    Only the admin can change them. Ask your department admin.

    How do I delete my account?

    Open your Profile and tap Delete account. See Delete your account.

    Getting the app

    Is it free?

    Yes. There are no ads, no subscriptions and nothing to buy.

    Is Mentor Heart on Google Play?

    Yes. Install it from Google Play. It updates itself through the Play Store.

    I installed the app from the APK file. Do I need Google Play?

    No. Your app keeps working and updates itself from Settings › Updates. If you want to move to the Play Store version, you must uninstall the old app first. Your records are safe on the server. Before you uninstall, save your Calm journal with Save a backup file, because the journal lives only on your phone.

    Android says the APK is from an unknown source. Is that safe?

    The APK is not from Google Play, so Android asks once. Download it only from the Mentor Heart website. Or use Google Play instead.

    The APK says “App not installed” or “Invalid package”.

    The app needs Android 7.0 or newer. It also can’t replace a Mentor Heart installed from Google Play. Or the download may be broken: download it again in Chrome, not inside WhatsApp. See If the APK won’t install.

    Is there an iPhone app?

    Not in the App Store. Use the web app and add it to your Home Screen. See how.

    Can I use it on a computer?

    Yes. Open mh.inigo.co.in in any browser and sign in.

    How do updates come?

    Small updates come by themselves. The app asks you to restart, then shows What’s new. New versions come through Google Play. Your records stay.

    When something looks wrong

    Today shows the wrong day order.

    The day order comes from the college calendar and the admin. Ask your department admin to check More › Day order. Pull down on Today to load the newest copy.

    My timetable is empty.

    Your class has no timetable yet, or you have no class. Students: ask your admin to check your class. Professors: you may not be linked to the staff list. See Your own hours.

    A class is cancelled but the app still shows it.

    The professor or the admin must cancel it in the app for that day. See Change a class for one day.

    The app shows old information.

    Pull down to refresh, or open Settings › Sync now. Without internet, the app shows the last copy you saw, with a note that says how old it is.

    I can’t see my mentor.

    The admin has not given you a mentor yet. Ask the department office.

    I can’t see the Department dashboard.

    Only admins and the head of department have it. If you are the head, ask your admin or the app’s owner to turn on Head of department for you. See Name a head of department.

    As head of department, can I approve students or edit the timetable?

    No. Head of department opens the dashboard only. For other tools, the admin must also turn on Admin for you.

    Why does the dashboard say “Not shared” for a student?

    That student has turned off My mentor can see it in My attendance. Their attendance stays private.

    Notifications

    I don’t get notifications.

    Open Settings › Notifications and tap Send a test notification. If it doesn’t come, allow notifications for Mentor Heart in your phone’s settings. See Notifications don’t arrive.

    I get too many notifications.

    Turn off the kinds you don’t want in Settings › Notifications.

    I don’t get notifications on my iPhone.

    Open the app from your Home Screen, not from Safari. Then turn on notifications in Settings. See Notifications on an iPhone.

    Assignments, leave and files

    My file won’t upload.

    A PDF must be 5 MB or smaller. Photos are made smaller by the app. Check your internet and try again. For big files, ask your professor if you can share a Google Drive link.

    I handed in the wrong file.

    You can change it until your professor marks it Seen. After that, ask your professor to tap Please redo.

    My hand-in file is gone.

    Files are deleted a month after they are marked, to keep the app free. Your marks and the record stay. Keep your own copy of your work.

    Can I cancel a leave application?

    Yes, while it is waiting. You can also withdraw approved leave before it starts. See Leave.

    Privacy

    Can my classmates see my marks or leave?

    No. Students only see their own records.

    Can the admin read my mentor’s notes about me?

    No. A mentor’s private notes are seen only by that mentor. You see the meeting summary.

    Who sees my Calm journal?

    Only you. It stays on your phone and is never sent anywhere.

    I share a phone with someone. What should I do?

    Turn on the app lock in Settings › App lock. Sign out from More when you are done.
