Privacy policy

Mentor Heart keeps what your department needs for mentoring and academic work, shows it only to the people in your department who need it, and nothing else. No ads, no tracking.

Updated 19 November 2026

Who runs it

Mentor Heart is made by Inigo, who teaches at St. Joseph’s College (Autonomous), Tiruchirappalli. Each department that uses it has its own admin, a professor of that department, who approves who may join. Questions about this policy go to inigo@tosstheworld.org.

What it keeps

When you register, you give:

  • your name, your D.No. or staff ID, and your department and class;
  • your mobile number, and an email address if you choose to;
  • for students, your date of birth, and a parent’s or guardian’s name and number (needed if you are under 18);
  • for professors, your designation and staff room;
  • a photo and a few lines about you, if you add them.

Then, as you use it, the app keeps what you and your department add: timetables and the calendar, announcements and poll answers, assignments, the files you hand in and the marks given, attendance, leave applications, meetings and their notes, tasks, goals and check-ins, certificates, event sign-ups and feedback, and, if you choose, that you are willing to give blood. To send notifications it keeps a token for your phone or browser.

You agree to this when you register: “my department keeps these details for mentoring and academic work.”

Who can see it

  • Only your department. Nobody in another department, or another college, can see anything of yours.
  • Students see their own records, their class’s timetable and posts, and their professors. They never see a classmate’s marks, attendance, leave, meetings or goals.
  • Professors and the admin see their department’s students and staff, and the records they need for teaching and mentoring.
  • A mentor’s meeting notes are read by that mentor alone. Not the student, not the admin.
  • Birthdays are shown only if you allow it in your profile, to classmates, to the department, or to no one.
  • Blood donors: if you say you are willing, the department’s professors and admin can see it with your name and phone. Students can’t.

Inigo, who runs the app, can reach the database. He uses that only to keep the app running and to help a department when it asks.

Where it is stored

Records and files are stored with Supabase, in its Mumbai region (India), and sent over encrypted connections. Every week a backup of the records is made to private storage, and the newest twelve are kept. Inigo may save copies of these backups to his own Google Drive, so a mistake can’t wipe a department’s year. Private meeting notes, passwords, notification tokens and the files themselves are never part of a backup.

Files handed in for an assignment are deleted 30 days after they are marked. The record that you handed in, and your marks, stay.

On your phone, the app keeps a copy of recent screens so Today, the timetable and the calendar open without a connection. Signing out clears it.

What it doesn’t do

  • No ads, and no advertising or analytics companies.
  • Your records are never sold, and never shared outside your department.
  • Nothing is used to profile you or train anything.

Deleting your account

In the app, open your Profile and choose Delete account.

  • A student’s profile is removed with everything linked to it: meetings, goals, check-ins, hand-ins and the rest.
  • A professor’s profile and private meeting notes are removed. Their mentees keep their own meeting records and tasks.
  • A department’s only admin must first make another professor admin.

It can’t be undone. A deleted account may stay in the weekly backups for about three months, and in copies Inigo has saved until he removes them. If you can’t sign in to delete it, ask your department admin, who can remove an account, or write to Inigo.

Your department may also keep its own records about you, such as attendance registers and marks, under the college’s rules. Deleting your Mentor Heart account doesn’t change those.

Children

Mentor Heart is for college students and staff. A student under 18 gives a parent’s or guardian’s number when registering, so their mentor can reach the family.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version goes here with a new date, and anything important is announced in the app.
